Pages, watermarks and security

Organising pages#

Most of these are on the Home ribbon, in All tools > Organize pages, and on the right-click menu of the page thumbnails:

  • rotate one page or all of them
  • move pages up and down, or drag thumbnails into a new order
  • insert blank pages, duplicate, delete a page or a range
  • extract a page or a range as a new PDF
  • merge other PDFs in, insert one at a chosen position, or split every page into its own file
  • crop pages by setting a margin for each edge

Compare PDFs shows the text that was added or removed between two files, page by page.

Watermarks and backgrounds#

Watermark opens a dialog with a live preview of the current page. You can set:

  • Text or image: preset words such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or COPY, or your own text with a font, size, colour and opacity, or a logo or scanned image
  • Angle: diagonal from corner to corner (the default) or any angle you like
  • Position: centred, at the top, at the bottom, or tiled across the whole page
  • Layer: behind the text and form fields as a background, or on top of everything
  • Pages: all, the current page, or a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-

To change a watermark, open Watermark again and apply the new settings: the new one replaces the old one on those pages. Remove Watermark takes it out again. Both actions can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

Headers, footers and numbering#

  • Header / Footer: a line of text at the top or bottom of every page
  • Page Numbers: "Page N of M" in the footer, left, centre or right
  • Bates Numbers: sequential numbers with a prefix, suffix and padding, used for legal work

Exporting#

You can export pages as PNG images, all the text as a .txt file, a basic Word document (text only, no layout), or a PDF/A copy for archiving. Compress saves a smaller copy and tells you how much it saved.

Passwords and redaction#

Password Protect encrypts the file with AES-256. You can set a password to open it and a separate one for permissions such as printing and copying. Remove Password strips the encryption from a file you've already unlocked. PdfEdit never stores the passwords you type.

Redact lets you draw black boxes over anything sensitive. Nothing is removed until you choose Apply Redactions, which deletes the content underneath for good.

Document details#

Document Properties edits the title, author, subject and keywords. The side panels show the bookmarks (outline) and any attached files, which you can extract, add or remove.
