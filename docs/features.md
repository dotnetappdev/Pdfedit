# Features

[← Back to README](../README.md)

![PdfEdit filling in a form](screenshots/demo-v4.gif)

- **Fill & Sign**: type into any form, even flat PDFs with no fields, and add signatures, dates, ticks and stamps. Sign with a certificate too.
- **Forms**: create fields by hand or let Detect Fields find them, with number formats and totals that add themselves up.
- **Review**: highlights, underline, squiggly and strikethrough, sticky notes, drawing, links, stamps (Acrobat's standard set plus your own, with a default), redaction and visual compare.
- **Pages**: rotate, reorder, merge, split, resize (A4 ↔ Letter, margins), watermark, number, convert to greyscale, password-protect and clean up scans.
- **Edit images**: move, resize, replace, save, copy or delete the pictures already in a PDF; snapshot any area as a picture.
- **Printing**: pages per sheet (2 to 16) and booklets to fold.
- **Import and export**: open Word files (with or without Office), Excel, PowerPoint, Google Docs, text, Markdown and HTML; save to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML, Markdown or ePub.
- **Cloud**: import from and save to Google Drive or OneDrive with your own app details.
- **Automation**: batch-process a folder, fill a form from every row of a spreadsheet, search a folder of PDFs.
- **Themes**: follows your Windows light, dark or contrast theme, or pick Office, Dracula, Nord, One Dark, Monokai, Solarized or GitHub Light.
- **Reading**: night mode, two-page view, slide show, read aloud and auto-scroll.
- **Accessibility**: adjustable text and icon sizes, screen reader announcements, a built-in voice that reads out what you're doing (and the tour), a high-contrast focus ring, reduced motion and adjustable timings. Preferences can be backed up and restored.
- **Optional AI**: ask about the document or any passage, get an outline or a translated copy, and let it fill fields or run commands. Use your own Claude or OpenAI key, or a free local model.
