Lots of forms are just lines and boxes printed on the page, or a scan of a paper form. You can't click into them like a normal form, but PdfEdit can still fill them in.

## Open the form

Open the PDF (**Ctrl+O**, or drag it onto the window). If clicking the boxes does nothing, the form has no fields. Carry on below.

## Add text in a box

1. On the **Fill & Sign** tab, choose **Add Text** (or press **T**).
2. Click *inside* a box printed on the page. PdfEdit finds the box and lines your text up with it.
3. Type. Click somewhere else when you're done.

A small toolbar sits above the selected text:

- the **grip** to drag it
- **A / A** to make the text smaller or larger
- **Fit**: grow the box, wrap the text, or keep a fixed size when the text is too long
- character spacing, handy for forms with one box per letter
- colours

Font, bold, italic and alignment are in the **Properties** panel.

![The text toolbar with the Fit menu open](../../../docs/screenshots/fill-text-fit.png)

## Tick boxes and mark choices

Click inside a small square box with **Add Text** or **Select** and PdfEdit puts a tick there.

For other marks, use the **Marks** group on the Fill & Sign tab: tick (✓), cross (✕), dot (●), circle (○) and a short line. Click to place, then resize with the corner handle.

![Ticks and crosses on a printed checklist](../../../docs/screenshots/fill-marks.png)

## Date and sign

Use **Date** to stamp today's date and **Sign** for your signature, as in [Fill in and sign a PDF form](fill-and-sign-a-form.md).

## Save

**Flatten & Save** is best for flat forms: it fixes your text into the page so it prints and displays the same everywhere.

> **Tip:** filling in the same flat form often? Make it fillable once with [Detect Fields](make-a-form-fillable.md). After that, you just click and type.
