## Open the first PDF

Open the document you want to start with. Its pages appear as **thumbnails** down the left side. If you can't see them, click **Thumbnails** on the View tab.

## Merge other PDFs in

Click **Merge PDFs** (Home tab, Pages group) and pick the other files. They're added after the last page, in the order you choose them.

To put a file somewhere else, use **Insert PDF** and choose where it goes.

## Put the pages in order

- **Drag** a thumbnail up or down to move the page.
- Or select a page and use **Move Up** / **Move Down**.
- Right-click a thumbnail to rotate, duplicate or delete it.

## Remove pages you don't need

Select the page and click **Delete Page**, or use **Delete Range** for several, for example `3-5, 9`.

**Ctrl+Z** brings back anything you deleted by mistake.

## Rotate sideways pages

Right-click the thumbnail and rotate it, or use the **View** tab: **Rotate CW** / **Rotate CCW**, or **All Pages** to turn the whole document.

## Take pages out, or split the file

- **Extract Page** or **Extract Range** saves the chosen pages as a new PDF and leaves the original alone.
- **Split PDF** saves every page as its own file.

## Save

**Save** (**Ctrl+S**), or **Save As** to keep the original as it was.
