## Set your name

Comments carry the author's name, so the other person knows who said what. Set yours in **Settings** before you start.

## Highlight and mark text

On the **Tools** tab:

- **Highlight** (press **I**): drag over the text. There are several highlight colours.
- **Underline**, **Squiggly** and **Strikethrough** work the same way.
- **Insert Text** marks where something is missing, and **Replace Text** suggests new wording for the words you drag across.

These are standard PDF comments, so Acrobat and other readers show them too.

## Add sticky notes

Choose **Sticky Note** and click the page where the comment belongs. Type your comment. Notes can have their own colour.

## Draw and point things out

The drawing tools are on the Tools tab: **Freehand**, **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Arrow**, **Line**, **Cloud** (a revision cloud) and **Callout** (a box with a pointer). The **Eraser** removes any drawing you click.

To measure on plans and drawings, use **Distance**, **Perimeter** and **Area**.

## Stamp it

Pick a stamp from the list, such as **Approved**, **Draft**, **For Comment** or **Not Approved**, and click the page. The **Dynamic** stamps add your name and the time underneath.

**Stamps...** shows every stamp with a preview, and **New Stamp...** makes your own in any colour.

![Stamps on a page](../../../docs/screenshots/fill-stamps.png)

## Use the Comments panel

Open **Comments** in the side panel. It lists every note, highlight and mark in page order. You can:

- click one to jump to it
- **reply**, so a conversation stays together
- set a **status**: Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled or Completed
- tick it off as done

## Send your comments

- **Save** the PDF and send it. Your comments travel inside the file.
- Or export just the comments: **Export XFDF** (View tab, or **Ctrl+Shift+E**). The other person imports them into their copy with **Import XFDF**. Acrobat and Foxit use the same format.
- **Ann. Summary** makes a list of every comment, for a meeting or a spreadsheet.
