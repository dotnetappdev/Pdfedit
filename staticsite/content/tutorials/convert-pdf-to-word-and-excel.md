## PDF to Word

1. Open the PDF.
2. Click **Export to Word** (Edit tab).
3. Choose where to save the `.docx`. On the web it downloads.

You get the PDF's text, one paragraph per line and a page break between pages, ready to edit in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs. The layout and pictures aren't copied. To keep the look of the page, use **Export as Images**, or edit the PDF itself with **Add Text** and **Edit Images**.

> **Tip:** a scanned PDF has no real text yet. Run OCR first. See [Scan paper into a searchable PDF](scan-to-a-searchable-pdf.md).

## PDF to Excel

**Export to Excel** (Edit tab) rebuilds the tables in the PDF as rows and columns, with amounts as real numbers you can add up. It's ideal for bank statements, invoices and price lists.

## PDF to PowerPoint and more

- **Export to PowerPoint**: one slide per page.
- **Export Text**: all the text as a `.txt` file.
- **Export to Other**: a web page, Markdown or an ePub e-book.
- **Export as Images**: every page as PNG or JPEG.
- **Extract Images**: the pictures inside the PDF.
- **Archive (PDF/A)**: a copy for long-term archiving.

## Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF

Just open the file in PdfEdit: **Open** on Windows, or **From Office** on Mac and the web. PdfEdit turns it into a PDF and opens it in a new tab.

- **Word** works without Office. If the document is a form, its boxes and blanks become fillable fields.
- **Excel and PowerPoint** need Microsoft Office or the free [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) on Windows, or LibreOffice on the server for the web version.
- **Google Docs, Sheets and Slides**: use **From Google Docs** (Windows) and paste the link, or open them through **Import from Cloud**.

Text files, Markdown, web pages and pictures open as PDFs too.
