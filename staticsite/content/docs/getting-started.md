# Getting started

A quick look round PdfEdit and the first things most people do with it. The Windows app, the Mac app and the web version share the same layout, so this page applies to all three. Where they differ, it says so.

![PdfEdit with a form open](../../../docs/screenshots/blazor/theme-light.png)

## Opening a PDF

- Click **Open** on the Home tab, or press **Ctrl+O** (**⌘O** on a Mac).
- Drag one or more PDFs onto the window. Each opens in its own tab.
- Double-click a PDF in Explorer or Finder, or use **Open with > PdfEdit**.
- **From Link** opens a PDF from a web address, and **Import from Cloud** opens one from Google Drive or OneDrive.

Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, Markdown, HTML and pictures open too. They're turned into a PDF first. See [Import, export and cloud](import-export-and-cloud.md).

> **Tip:** with nothing open, the start page lists your recent files and offers the templates, so you can start a new document straight away.

## The window

| Part | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Ribbon** (top) | The tools, grouped into tabs: Home, Fill & Sign, Edit, View, Tools, Forms, AI Assistant, Design and Help |
| **Document tabs** | One for every open file. Ctrl+Tab moves between them and Ctrl+W closes one |
| **Thumbnails** (left) | Every page. Click to go there, drag to reorder, right-click to rotate, duplicate or delete |
| **Page area** (middle) | Your document. **Live View** shows the PDF; **Design** is the layout canvas |
| **Side panel** (right) | Properties of what's selected, the form's Fields, Comments, Bookmarks, Search, the AI chat and All tools |
| **Status bar** (bottom) | The page you're on, zoom, how much of a form is filled in, and messages |

Not sure where something is? Open **All tools** in the side panel. It lists every tool by task (Fill & sign, Organize pages, Protect, Convert, Scan & OCR and more) and has its own search.

## The things most people do first

1. **Fill in a form**: click a field and type. On a form with no fields, use **Add Text** on the Fill & Sign tab and click where the text should go. [Fill and sign a form](fill-and-sign-a-form.md) walks through it.
2. **Sign**: Fill & Sign > **Sign** > **New Signature...** to draw, type or import your signature once. After that it's one click.
3. **Comment**: Tools tab > Highlight, Sticky Note, Draw or Stamp. See [Review a document](review-a-document.md).
4. **Organise pages**: drag thumbnails, or use the Pages group on the Home tab to merge, split, extract or delete. See [Merge, split and reorder pages](merge-split-and-reorder.md).
5. **Save**: **Ctrl+S** keeps form fields editable; **Flatten & Save** fixes everything in place for the copy you send.

## Saving your work

- **Windows and Mac**: Save writes back to the file, and Save As asks where with your system's own dialog.
- **Web**: Save downloads the finished PDF. While you work, PdfEdit keeps a **draft** in your browser: open documents, things you've placed but not applied yet, typed values and the Design canvas. If the page reloads or the connection drops, everything comes back. **Save Draft** keeps it straight away.

Every change can be undone with **Ctrl+Z** until you save.

## Getting help inside PdfEdit

- **Help > User Guide** opens this guide, and **Help > Tutorials** opens the [tutorials](../tutorials/index.html).
- **Help > Take the Tour** walks round the main parts of the window.
- **Help > Tip of the Day** shows a useful tip, one at a time.
- **F1** lists every keyboard shortcut. You can change them in Settings.
- Hover over any button to see what it does and its shortcut.

## Making it comfortable

**Settings** (Home tab, or File > Settings on Windows) has your theme, interface size and text size, accessibility options, AI providers, OCR languages and cloud accounts. See [Themes](themes.md) and [Accessibility](accessibility.md).
