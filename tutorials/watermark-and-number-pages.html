Add a watermark, page numbers and Bates numbers

Stamp DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL across your pages, add a header and footer, and number the pages.

Level Beginner Time 5 minutes Works in WindowsMacWeb

Add a watermark#

  1. Click Watermark (Home tab, Pages group; also next to the stamps on Fill & Sign and Tools).
  2. Type the text, such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or COPY. On Windows you can also pick these from a list, or use a picture such as a logo.
  3. Set the font, size, colour and opacity. Around 20 to 30% is light enough to read through.
  4. Choose the layout (diagonal or horizontal) and position (centre, top, bottom, or tiled across the page).
  5. Tick Behind the page content to put it behind the text, like a background.
  6. Choose All pages, This page or a range such as 1-3, 5.
  7. Click Add watermark.

To change it (a bigger size, other text or colour), add the watermark again: the new one replaces the old one. Remove Watermark takes it off again, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.

TipA stamp you've made can also be used as a page background, under Stamps....

Add page numbers#

Click Page Numbers and choose where they go: left, centre or right of the footer. PdfEdit writes "Page 1 of 12" and so on.

Add a header or footer#

Click Header / Footer (Home tab, Document group) and type the text for the top or bottom of every page, for example the document name and date.

Add Bates numbers#

Legal documents are often numbered right through a set of files. Click Bates Numbers and set:

  • a prefix, such as SMITH-
  • the starting number
  • how many digits (for example 000001)
  • an optional suffix

Save#

Save keeps the changes. Everything can be undone with Ctrl+Z until then.
