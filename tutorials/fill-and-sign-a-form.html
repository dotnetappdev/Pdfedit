Fill in and sign a PDF form

Type into a form, tick the boxes, add today's date and your signature, then save a copy to send.

Level Beginner Time 5 minutes Works in WindowsMacWeb

Before you start#

You need a PDF form, for example one you were emailed or downloaded. To practise, use any form from the Samples folder in the PdfEdit download, or make one from a template: File > New from Template (on Windows), or New on the start page (on Mac and the web).

Open the form#

Open the PDF with Open on the Home tab (Ctrl+O), or drag it onto the PdfEdit window.

Click Highlight Fields on the Fill & Sign tab to shade every field so the empty ones stand out. Required fields get a red outline. (On Windows, a bar across the top of a form offers the same as Highlight Existing Fields.)

TipNothing highlighted? The form is probably flat: boxes printed on the page with no fields. Follow Fill in a form that has no fields instead.

Type your answers#

  • Click a field and type. Tab moves to the next field and Shift+Tab goes back.
  • Click a check box or radio button anywhere inside it to tick it.
  • Date fields have a small calendar button.
  • Drop-down fields show their choices when you click them.

The status bar shows how much of the form is filled in, with a button that jumps to the next empty field.

Add the date#

On the Fill & Sign tab, choose Date and click where the date should go. PdfEdit writes today's date.

To change the format, select the date and pick another in the Properties panel, for example dd/MM/yyyy, MM/dd/yyyy or 4 October 2026.

Create your signature (once)#

On the Fill & Sign tab, click New Signature... and choose how to make it:

  • Draw it with your mouse, trackpad, pen or finger
  • Type your name and pick a handwriting-style font
  • use a photo or scan of your signature. PdfEdit removes the paper behind the ink.

Your signature is saved for next time. Do the same with New Initials... if the form asks for initials.

Sign#

Click Sign and then click the signature line. Drag the signature to move it, and use the corner handle to resize it.

NoteThis puts a picture of your signature on the page, like signing on paper. If someone asks for a digital signature, use Certificate... on the same tab instead. It needs a Digital ID (.pfx file).

Check you haven't missed anything#

Use Validate (Forms tab on Windows; Edit > Validate Required on Mac and the web). It lists any required fields that are still empty and jumps to the first one.

Save and send#

  • Save (Ctrl+S) keeps the fields editable, so you can change them later.
  • Flatten & Save fixes everything into the page so it can't be changed, and looks the same in every PDF reader. Use it for the copy you send.

On the web, Save downloads the finished PDF. Share (Fill & Sign tab) sends it through your phone's or computer's share sheet.
