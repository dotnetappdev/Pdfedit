Design a document from a template

Start from one of 70 templates, make it your own on the Design canvas and export it as a fillable PDF.

Level Beginner Time 10 minutes Works in WindowsMacWeb

The Design canvas is a blank page for laying out invoices, forms, letters, certificates and more. The templates give you a head start.

Open the template gallery#

Choose File > New from Template on Windows, or New on the start page (Mac and web). You can also use All Templates on the Design tab.

Browse by category (Business, Forms, Agreements, Letters & office, Resumes, Marketing & events, Certificates & cards, Education, Planning & personal) or search. Click a template to see a large preview.

Choose how to start#

  • Customise in Design puts the template on the canvas so you can change it.
  • Open as PDF makes it a PDF straight away.

Templates marked Fillable have real form fields, so the PDF is ready to fill in, sign and send.

For this tutorial, choose Customise in Design.

Change the text#

Double-click any text to edit it in place: your company name, address, headings. Use the Text Format group for font, size, bold, colour and alignment.

Add your logo and other things#

From the Design tab's tools:

  • Image for your logo or a photo
  • Text, Rectangle, Ellipse, Line and Arrow
  • Table for rows and columns, with a styled header row
  • form fields (text box, check box, drop-down, signature and more) that become real PDF fields

Arrange it#

  • Drag to move, and drag the handles to resize. Hold Shift to keep the proportions.
  • Turn on Grid and Snap to line things up.
  • Select several items, then use Align, Space Across / Down and Same Size.
  • Lock anything you don't want to move by accident.
  • Ctrl+D duplicates and Ctrl+Z undoes.

Save the design#

Save Design keeps a design file you can open and change later with Load Design. On the web, your canvas is also kept in your browser's draft as you work.

Export a PDF#

  • Export PDF saves it as a PDF.
  • Open as PDF opens the result in Live View, where you can fill it in, sign it or send it.

What's next#

  • Not quite what you need? Try Design Form with AI on the Design tab: describe the form and PdfEdit lays it out. See Design canvas.
